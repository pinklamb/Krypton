Keep the reins on your code.

      
AI writes more of your code every day, and the companies selling it grade their own work. Krypton puts the grading back in your hands. Running locally on your machine, it scores how easy every change is for the next agent to edit, blocks messy merges, and cuts your AI coding bill by up to 35% through higher code quality and smarter prompt caching.

Set your rules in a local dashboard
Run krypton setup on your machine. It maps your codebase and shows its score today. Then set your target score, cap how many times the agent can retry, and choose whether it carries on by itself or pauses for your review before opening a pull request. Answer each question in the terminal, or open the link it gives you and click your settings on a local dashboard.
Krypton suggests a target a step above where you are: if your codebase scores 56, it suggests 70, and you can pick your own. Only new tickets are held to it, so the code they touch gets cleaner while old code never blocks a merge. Run setup again as the score climbs and the suggestion climbs with it.

Each developer keeps full control of their own local limits and agent loops, and old code never blocks anyone.
