The compiler for AI agents

Your AI agents re‑read your whole codebase every turn. Krypton makes that cheaper.

Krypton keeps the files that rarely change at the front of every request, so the AI provider can reuse more of what it has already read. A 25,000-line codebase is roughly 175,000 tokens, read again on every turn.
