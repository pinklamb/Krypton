# Krypton review dashboard style

When your coding agent has finished its runs, it builds a small review page on your computer so you can approve the work before anything
goes to GitHub. It builds that page from this file, so write it the way you would brief a designer. Use your own words. Anything you leave
blank, the agent decides.

## The look

This file only changes how your review page looks. The page always shows the same things: your ticket, your code's health score, and how your decision changes that score.

Warm and simple. A soft blush background, white cards and a dark red accent. Calm, not flashy.

## Colors

- Background: soft blush (#f7eded)
- Cards: white
- Text: very dark wine (#2b0f14)
- Accent, for buttons and bars: dark red (#9b1c2e)
- Good news: green. Needs attention: amber.

## Fonts and shape

Clean sans-serif text. Rounded corners, a light shadow and comfortable spacing.

## Wording

- Page title: Krypton review
- Buttons: "Approve best", "Send back", "Finish"

## Anything else

Leave this blank, or add anything you like, for example "big text, I read this on my phone" or "no animation".
